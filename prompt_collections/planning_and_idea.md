# Planning Phase

## Socratic Questioning

Do not rush to answer, and do not assume I have fully thought this problem through.

Please engage me in a strict "Socratic Inquiry" regarding my current problem or idea:

Briefly reflect back the core premise or goal you understand me to be aiming for, without attempting to solve it.

Identify the most critical underlying assumption, vague definition, or potential blind spot in my premise.

Ask me exactly one precise, open-ended question designed to force me to explain my underlying logic, define my terms, or confront that specific blind spot.

Wait for my response. Do not provide a definitive judgment, comprehensive analysis, or actionable next steps yet. After I answer, evaluate my reasoning and ask the next logical Socratic question to dig one layer deeper. We will repeat this continuous loop until we reach the fundamental truth or until I explicitly ask for your final judgment.

### End

I have the clarity I need. Please terminate the Socratic questioning. Now, compile a final architectural summary and next steps. Structure this summary strictly using the Minto Pyramid Principle: start with the single core objective at the top, followed by the supporting technical pillars, and finally the granular execution details at the base.

## Steelman

Do not rush to answer, and do not assume I have fully thought this problem through.

Please first conduct a detailed "Two-Way Steelman Analysis" of this issue:

Restate the core problem I am genuinely trying to solve in the most comprehensive and compelling way possible.

Apply the Steelman technique to construct the strongest possible arguments supporting my current perspective, followed by the strongest possible arguments opposing it.

Identify the fundamental divergence between these two sides, along with the key variables most likely to alter the final conclusion.

Ask me exactly one critical clarifying question.

Wait for my response to that question. Only then should you provide a definitive judgment, your rationale, and actionable next steps.
